Common Online Threats

Online threats are dangers that can affect internet users, devices, and personal information.

Phishing

Phishing uses deceptive messages to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

Malware

Malware is harmful software that can affect computers and other devices.

Ransomware

Ransomware encrypts a user's data and demands a payment for decryption.

Social engineering

Social engineering manipulates people into divulging confidential information or performing actions that compromise security.

Denial of Service (DoS)

DoS attacks aim to make a network or service unavailable by overwhelming it with traffic or requests.
